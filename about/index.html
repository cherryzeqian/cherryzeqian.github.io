Nice to meet you!

I’m Zeqian, a Product and UX Designer who creates clear, data-informed experiences across digital platforms, service flows, and interactive products.

With a background in photography and video, I bring a strong sense of visual storytelling into my design process. I’m especially interested in how research, behavior data, and interface structure can help reduce user uncertainty and make complex decisions feel easier.

My experience spans both large-scale platforms and startup environments, including work on mobile gaming, film equipment rental, financial behavior, and music experiences. Across these projects, I focus on turning messy user journeys into intuitive, purposeful product experiences.

Outside of design, I enjoy shooting film photography and exploring how visual details shape emotion, memory, and attention.
