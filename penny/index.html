Helping you stay mindful with your money.

We designed Penny because... 💰

Many young adults download financial apps with good intentions.

They want to save more, spend smarter, and feel in control.

But budgeting often starts to feel cold, stressful, and easy to avoid.

Penny turns these stressful moments into small chances to pause, reflect, and choose with intention.

The solution 🎨

Penny. is a budgeting assistant that helps young adults make spending decisions with more awareness.

Penny turns budgeting from passive tracking into active reflection through priorities, tickets, trade-offs, and goal progress.

Penny is a personal finance app concept designed for people who want clarity without shame. Instead of “strict budgeting”, the experience nudges users toward mindful spending through lightweight tracking, reflection, and friendly visual feedback.

This case study covers my design goals, key UX decisions, and how the interface supports small, repeatable habits.

Role & scope
End-to-end product design.

My role

UX, UI, interaction design, prototyping.

Deliverables

Core flows, key screens, component styling.

Constraints

Fast iteration, clear hierarchy, calm tone.

Audience

People who want to build money habits with low friction.

Approach
Design for gentle consistency.
  • Reduce friction by prioritizing quick logging and clear defaults.
  • Reframe feedback around reflection and progress, not punishment.
  • Keep it calm with warm color and generous whitespace.
  • Show only what matters through strong hierarchy and progressive disclosure.
Key screens
A small set of helpful moments.
Outcome
A calm, encouraging prototype.

The resulting direction emphasizes clarity, warmth, and minimal effort. The UI aims to feel approachable—something users can come back to daily without dread.
