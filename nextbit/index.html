NEXTBIT — Redesigning a Film Equipment Rental Platform

8-bit Rental specializes in film equipment rental and sales. Through design research, I identified challenges like complex selection processes, lack of real-time inventory, and weak brand presence.


I created a user-focused interface that simplifies selection, adds real-time tracking, and strengthens brand identity. This led to improved brand recognition, higher conversion rates, optimized MVP, and increased purchasing power through bundles and loyalty rewards, ultimately boosting customer satisfaction and market competitiveness.

The Challenge

8-bit Rental is a New York–based film equipment startup offering affordable, flexible rentals for creators. As the company expanded, its digital presence failed to communicate its value clearly.

Key problems:

  • Low brand recognition

  • Complex equipment selection process

  • No real-time inventory visibility

  • Fragmented web and mobile experience

Industry Research

A Rental company offers a wide range of cameras, lenses, and filmmaking equipment for rent.

Lack of Specialty Equipment: Focuses on standard filmmaking tools but lacks advanced options like robotic arms or specialized lighting systems.

Limited Collaboration Opportunities: Does not offer programs or incentives for young creators or partnerships with artists.

Generic User Experience: Provides efficient rental services but lacks a unique user experience tailored for creativity and experimentation.

Narrow Target Audience: Primarily targets small teams and independent creators, leaving gaps for larger-scale productions or corporate clients.


No Brand Community: Does not actively promote collaboration, loyalty programs, or a creative community for users to engage with.

User Research

A significant gap exists between film equipment demand and affordability for underfunded creators. High rental costs, insurance, and deposits create barriers, limiting access. Solutions like subsidies, student discounts, or community initiatives could promote inclusivity and innovation in NYC's film industry.

The interviews highlight diverse user needs across professional filmmakers, corporate clients, and student filmmakers, revealing key priorities for a rental platform like 8-bit Rental:

Availability and Reliability: Professional users prioritize dependable equipment that is ready and functional for short-term shoots, where timing is critical.


Streamlined Coordination: Corporate clients value tools like dashboards for tracking orders, saving custom equipment packages, and corporate invoicing to simplify large-scale operations.


Affordability and Accessibility: Student filmmakers seek affordable pricing, student discounts, and convenient delivery or pick-up options to manage tight budgets and deadlines.

Future Strategic Analysis

The future of 8-bit Rental lies in transitioning from a traditional equipment rental company to a creative platform that empowers young creators and artists. This shift involves offering accessible high-end tools, fostering collaborations with creatives, and developing a community-focused ecosystem.

Current Model

Renting and selling film equipment.

Future Model

Providing affordable access to high-end equipment, mentorship programs, collaborative projects, and content creation spaces.
